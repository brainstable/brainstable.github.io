Applications

MeteoGSU · sown areas · reference · statistics

Four desktop applications built for variety testing and agronomy. Some of them are used at a branch of the state variety-testing commission and at Krasnoyarsk State Agrarian University. Screenshots come from real work — browse with the arrows, the keyboard or a swipe. The application interface is in Russian.

MeteoGSU

weather data analysis and visualisation

A desktop application for weather data tied to trial sites. Data is presented by month and decade across six indicators: average, maximum and minimum temperature, precipitation, snow depth and dew point. It calculates the hydrothermal coefficient, dates when temperature crosses 0, 5, 10 and 15 °C, temperature and precipitation sums, the frost-free period, shows tables and charts, merges rp5 archives and exports results to Excel.

  • C#
  • WinForms
  • Excel
  • rp5
