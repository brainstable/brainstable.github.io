03 — Approach

Data → documents → maps

Three things that repeat in almost every project.

01

Data

CSV in CP1251, Excel, XML and GeoJSON. Loading, deduplicated merging, mapping to models and calculations: hydrothermal coefficient, temperature and precipitation sums, areas, statistics.

02

Documents

Word templates and the Open XML SDK: section tables, title page, table of contents, merging and continuous pagination. No installed Word required for generation.

03

Maps

Leaflet and GeoJSON: layers of sites, fields and roads, switching basemaps Google / Yandex / Bing / ESRI / OSM, legends and coordinate binding.
